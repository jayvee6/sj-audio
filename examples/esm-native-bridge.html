SJAudio — Native bridge (system audio, every browser)

Captures whatever audio is playing on the machine via SJAudioBridge (the notarized macOS menubar helper) over a token-gated localhost WebSocket. Works in Safari/Firefox/Chrome alike — the page just reads a socket.
1. Launch SJAudioBridge. 2. Menubar ▸ Copy Connection Token. 3. Paste below. 4. Connect. 5. Play music anywhere.
